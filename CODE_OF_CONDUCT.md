# 屿阅社区行为准则 / Yuyue Code of Conduct

## 我们的承诺 / Our pledge

屿阅社区希望为不同背景、经验和身份的参与者提供安全、尊重、友善的协作环境。参与本项目即表示同意以专业、建设性的方式交流。

The Yuyue community aims to provide a safe, respectful, and welcoming collaboration environment for people of all backgrounds, experience levels, and identities. By participating, you agree to communicate professionally and constructively.

## 期待的行为 / Expected behavior

- 尊重不同观点和使用场景。

  Respect different viewpoints and use cases.
- 提供具体、可验证、针对工作的反馈，不攻击个人。

  Give specific, verifiable, work-focused feedback without attacking people.
- 主动说明不确定性、利益冲突和错误，并愿意修正。

  State uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and mistakes openly, and be willing to correct them.
- 保护漏洞报告者、用户和贡献者的隐私。

  Protect the privacy of vulnerability reporters, users, and contributors.
- 在讨论中关注项目目标、用户影响和共同利益。

  Focus discussions on project goals, user impact, and the common good.

## 不可接受的行为 / Unacceptable behavior

- 骚扰、威胁、歧视、侮辱、跟踪或带有性意味的不当表达。

  Harassment, threats, discrimination, insults, stalking, or unwelcome sexualized language.
- 人身攻击、恶意挑衅或持续破坏讨论。

  Personal attacks, bad-faith provocation, or persistent disruption of discussions.
- 未经许可公开他人的私人信息。

  Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- 故意公开尚未修复的漏洞细节，或利用漏洞伤害他人。

  Intentionally publishing details of an unfixed vulnerability or using a vulnerability to harm others.
- 其他在专业开源协作中可合理认定为不当的行为。

  Other conduct that can reasonably be considered inappropriate in professional open-source collaboration.

## 适用范围 / Scope

本准则适用于仓库、Issue、PR、Discussion、Release、与项目相关的线上线下活动，以及代表屿阅社区的公开场合。

This code applies to the repository, Issues, PRs, Discussions, Releases, project-related online or offline events, and public spaces where someone represents the Yuyue community.

## 执行与报告 / Enforcement and reporting

维护者可以编辑、隐藏或移除违反本准则的内容，拒绝贡献，临时限制互动或永久禁止参与。措施应与影响相称，并尽可能说明原因。

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates this code, decline contributions, temporarily restrict interaction, or permanently ban participation. Measures should be proportionate to the impact, with reasons explained whenever possible.

请通过 [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) 所述的私密渠道报告行为事件，并在标题中标注 `Code of Conduct`。不要在公开 Issue 中披露当事人的私人信息。维护者必须保护报告者隐私并公平调查。

Report conduct incidents through the private channel described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), and include `Code of Conduct` in the subject. Do not disclose anyone's private information in a public Issue. Maintainers must protect reporters' privacy and investigate fairly.

This code is adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and simplified for the current size of the Yuyue community.
